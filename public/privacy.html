Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the FIT fitness companion handles information when you create and use an account.

Information you provide

FIT may store your name, email address, password hash, profile details, fitness goals, workout completion records, meal logs, hydration entries, body measurements and app preferences. Do not enter information you do not want stored.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate your account, save and synchronize your fitness logs, display your progress and provide features you request. If you use FIT Coach, your question is sent to the AI service configured by the app operator to generate a response.

Storage and security

Account and synchronized app data are stored in the PostgreSQL database configured by the operator. Passwords are hashed. Authentication uses signed session tokens. No internet service can guarantee absolute security; use a unique password and sign out on shared devices.

Sharing

FIT does not intentionally sell your personal information. Data may be processed by infrastructure and AI providers needed to operate features. The app operator should identify its actual hosting and AI providers before public launch.

Your choices

You can export your app data from Settings. You can request account deletion from Settings; deletion removes your account and linked database records when the request succeeds. Some provider logs or legally required records may be retained under the provider's policies.

Children and health

FIT is not designed for children under 13. Fitness information is sensitive personal information. Do not use FIT as a medical record or enter details you are uncomfortable sharing.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the FIT app operator using the contact details published with the official app listing. Those contact details must be added by the operator before public release.

This is a starter policy template and must be reviewed and completed with the operator's legal identity, contact details, providers and applicable law before launch.
